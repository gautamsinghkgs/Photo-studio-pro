Privacy Policy — Photo Studio Pro

Last updated: 29 October 2026  ·  Effective: 29 October 2026

The short version: your photos are processed entirely on your device. We have no server, no database and no upload endpoint, so there is nowhere for your photos to go. We do not require an account, an email address, or a phone number. The only personal data collected is advertising and analytics data handled by third-party SDKs, described in full below.

1. Who we are

Photo Studio Pro ("the App") is an Android application published on Google Play by [YOUR LEGAL NAME AS IT APPEARS IN PLAY CONSOLE] ("we", "us"). Contact: [YOUR-EMAIL@DOMAIN].

We operate no backend server. The App contains no code that transmits your photos, your contact list, your location, or any user-created content to us or to anyone else.

2. Photos and images

All image processing — background removal, face detection for ID photos, resizing, compression, collages, OCR and enhancement — runs locally on your device using Google ML Kit and the Android graphics framework.

  • Photos you select are read into your device's memory, processed, and written to a location you choose via the Android file picker. They are never sent to us.
  • Temporary copies may be written to the App's private cache directory to make an export possible. These are deleted by the App or by Android when storage is reclaimed.
  • The App excludes your exports from Android cloud backup, so processed photos are not copied to your Google account backup by us. (Your own choice to back up your device storage separately is outside our control.)
  • We have no ability to view, recover, or delete your photos, because we never receive them.

3. The one-time model download (important, and we are explicit about it)

The Background Remover uses Google's Subject Segmentation model, delivered through Google Play services rather than being bundled inside the App. On first use, the machine-learning model itself may be downloaded to your device over the internet by Google Play services.

Only the model travels. Your photos do not. Segmentation inference happens on your device after the model arrives. This is why we describe the App as working "offline after one-time setup" rather than claiming it needs no internet at all. The other tools work offline from the very first launch.

5. Data collected by third-party SDKs

The App embeds the following third-party SDKs. Each may collect data independently of us, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive their raw data.

5.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the User Messaging Platform

  • Purpose: to show advertising, and to record your ad-tracking consent choice.
  • May collect: advertising identifier, coarse/approximate location derived from IP address, device information, app information, ad interaction data (impressions and clicks), and diagnostic data.
  • Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Your control: on Android 13+ you can revoke the advertising identifier under Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland we request your consent through the Google User Messaging Platform before personalising ads; you can change that choice at any time under Settings → Personalisation & ads inside the App. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised.

5.2 Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics

  • Purpose: to understand which tools are used and to diagnose crashes.
  • May collect: app-instance identifier, usage events (which tool was opened, whether an export completed, whether an ad was shown), device model, OS version, app version, country, and crash stack traces.
  • Explicitly never collected: your photos, and any content of your photos. Crash reports do not contain image data.
  • Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.3 Google Firebase Remote Config

  • Purpose: to let us turn a feature or an ad format off without waiting for a Play Store update, and to tune ad frequency if it is harming your experience.
  • May collect: app-instance identifier and configuration fetch logs.

5.4 RevenueCat (purchases and subscriptions)

  • Purpose: to validate your Google Play purchase and to keep your Pro entitlement working, including offline.
  • May collect: an anonymous app-user identifier generated on your device, your Google Play purchase token, product identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, and country.
  • Explicitly never collected: your name, email, or payment card number. Google Play handles payment directly; the App and RevenueCat never see your card details.
  • Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

5.5 Google Play Services (ML Kit model delivery)

  • Purpose: to deliver the Subject Segmentation model described in section 3.
  • Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Advertising in detail

The App shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for a small temporary benefit such as removing the watermark from one export. Rewarded ads are always optional and always initiated by you.

We may also show interstitial ads after you complete and save an export. We have deliberately limited these: none appear during your first two days of use, none appear within the first 90 seconds of a session, none appear while you are setting up the App, at least three minutes pass between them, and no more than three appear per day. If you are a paying Pro subscriber, or are in a free trial, you will never see an interstitial ad.

Banner ads are permanently disabled in this App, to avoid accidental clicks.

7. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play, not by us. Your subscription automatically renews at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You can manage or cancel it at any time in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling does not remove access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refund requests are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme and does not use Families-certified ad formats. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

  • Photos: never retained by us, because never received.
  • Usage counters (exports today, ads shown today): stored on your device only, in the App's private storage, and reset daily. Cleared when you clear App data or uninstall.
  • Pro entitlement: cached on your device so Pro keeps working offline, and held by RevenueCat for as long as your Google Play purchase record exists.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google under its own retention settings.
